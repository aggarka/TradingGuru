Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I add stocks? Go to the Watchlist tab and enter a ticker symbol in the search box.
  • What do ORDER and HOLD mean? ORDER indicates a potential trading opportunity based on historical analysis. HOLD means the criteria aren't met.
  • How do I set up notifications? Go to Settings and configure your preferred analysis schedule and notification frequency.
