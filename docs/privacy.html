Introduction

TradingGuru ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Apple, we receive your Apple ID email address (or a relay email if you choose to hide it) and a unique user identifier.
  • Watchlist Data: Stock ticker symbols you add to your watchlist are stored to enable analysis features.
  • Preferences: Your notification settings and analysis schedule preferences.

Information Automatically Collected

  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app experience.
  • Device Information: Basic device type and operating system version for compatibility purposes.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain the TradingGuru service
  • Run scheduled analysis on your watchlist
  • Send notifications about trading opportunities (if enabled)
  • Improve and optimize the app experience

Data Storage

  • Your data is stored locally on your device and in Firebase (Google Cloud Platform)
  • We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data
  • Your watchlist and preferences are associated with your user account

Third-Party Services

TradingGuru uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication: For secure sign-in with Apple
  • Firebase Firestore: For storing user preferences and watchlist
  • Yahoo Finance API: For fetching market data (no personal data is shared)

Data Sharing

We do NOT:

  • Sell your personal information
  • Share your data with third parties for marketing purposes
  • Use your financial data for any purpose other than providing app features

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt out of notifications
  • Request a copy of your data

Data Retention

  • Your data is retained as long as your account is active
  • You can delete your account at any time through the app settings
  • Upon account deletion, all associated data is permanently removed

Children's Privacy

TradingGuru is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the app.
