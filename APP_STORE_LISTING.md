## App Description (4000 characters max)

TradingGuru is your intelligent companion for analyzing weekly options trading opportunities. Using a proven strategy based on historical price movements, TradingGuru helps you identify potential CALL and PUT opportunities with clear buy signals.

**KEY FEATURES**

📊 **Smart Watchlist Management**
Create and manage your personal watchlist of stocks. Simply add ticker symbols and TradingGuru does the rest.

📈 **Historical Analysis**
Analyze stocks using historical price data with configurable lookback periods (30-365 days). Our algorithm calculates optimal entry points based on past performance.

🎯 **Clear Trading Signals**
Get actionable ORDER or HOLD signals for both CALL and PUT opportunities. See strike prices, premiums, and return percentages at a glance.

⏰ **Scheduled Analysis**
Set up automatic analysis to run at your preferred times. Never miss a potential opportunity with customizable notification schedules.

🔔 **Smart Notifications**
Receive instant alerts when ORDER signals are detected. Configure notification frequency to suit your trading style.

💰 **Options Chain Integration**
View real-time bid/ask premiums, strike prices, and expiration dates. Filter by your preferred premium percentage and window period.

📅 **Earnings Awareness**
Stay informed about upcoming earnings dates. TradingGuru highlights when earnings fall within your option's expiration window so you can make informed decisions.

🔐 **Secure Sign In**
Use Sign in with Apple for fast, secure, and private authentication. Your data stays protected.

**CONFIGURABLE STRATEGY PARAMETERS**
• Window Days: 5, 7, 9, 14, 21, 28, or 30 days
• Lookback Period: 30, 60, 90, 180, or 365 days
• Premium Target: 0.5%, 1.0%, 1.5%, 2.0%, or 3.0%
• View all results or ORDER signals only

**RESULTS AT A GLANCE**
• Signal (ORDER/HOLD)
• Option Type (CALL/PUT)
• Expiration Date
• Strike Price
• Bid/Ask/Mid Premium
• Expected Return %
• Current vs Target Price
• Next Earnings Date

**DISCLAIMER**
TradingGuru is an educational and analysis tool only. It does not constitute financial advice. Options trading involves significant risk and is not suitable for all investors. Past performance does not guarantee future results. Always consult with a qualified financial advisor and understand the risks before trading.
